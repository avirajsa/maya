# Welcome to Maya 🌟

Your personal AI assistant — intelligent, capable, and always ready.

## What I can do

| Capability | How to use |
|---|---|
| 💬 **Chat** | Just type your message below |
| 🎤 **Voice** | Click the microphone icon |
| 🔢 **Calculate** | "What is 15% of 2,847?" |
| 🔍 **Search** | "Search for the latest AI news" |
| 💡 **Smart Home** | "Turn off the bedroom lights" |
| 📝 **Summarise** | "Summarise this article: [url]" |

## Tips
- I remember our conversation context — you can refer back to previous messages
- For voice, speak clearly and wait for the mic to stop recording
- Ask me anything — if I can't do it directly, I'll find a way
